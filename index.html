Right in the form

Click the mask in an email field. Maskwell creates the address, tags it with the site, fills it in and puts it on your clipboard.

If you already have an address for the site, Maskwell offers it before making another.

Wherever you need one

Widgets, Control Center, the Action button, the Share Sheet and Shortcuts on iPhone; the Services menu, AppleScript and the command line on the Mac.

Private by design

Maskwell talks only to Fastmail. Your sign-in stays in your device’s Keychain, and it can’t read your mail. We never see your addresses.
